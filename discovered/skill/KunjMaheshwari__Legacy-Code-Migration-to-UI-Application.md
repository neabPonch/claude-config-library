# Skill.md — Design & Technical Skills Demonstrated

A record of the design thinking and engineering decisions applied throughout this project.

---

## UI/UX Design Skills

### Color Theory & Mood
- Selected a **finance-grade palette** — deep navy (`#0f172a`) establishes trust and authority, the dominant emotion for banking products
- Used **blue (`#2563eb`) as the single accent** — universally associated with reliability and technology; avoids the visual noise of multi-color systems
- Deliberately **avoided red for action buttons** — red triggers alarm and implies danger; debit actions use a neutral outlined style instead
- Dashboard background uses **slate-50 (`#f8fafc`)** rather than pure white — reduces eye strain while maintaining a clean, professional surface

### Layout & Composition
- Applied the **split-panel login pattern** (used by Linear, Vercel, Stripe) — left panel anchors brand identity, right panel handles the task
- Used **visual hierarchy through whitespace** — generous padding, breathing room between elements, and consistent `gap` values prevent visual clutter
- Action tiles on the home screen follow the **Gestalt principle of similarity** — identical size, bord
